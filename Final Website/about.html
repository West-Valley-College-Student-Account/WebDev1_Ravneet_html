    • About

    Rav Studio was concieved in 2025 in Saratoga, California. The designer behind it is Ravneet Mavi, a rare blend of an artist and a technologist with over a decade of experience in software product development behind her.

    This portfolio showcases a range of projects, including illustrations and designs created using tools such as Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and Microsoft Visio. Each piece reflects a moment in her evolving journey, with timestamps that highlight her progression and continuous refinement of craft.

    The goal is to provide insight into Ravneet's design approach - blending technical precision with artistic vision. The work presented here serves both as a foundation for professional opportunities and as an ongoing exploration of creative expression, demonstrating designer's adaptability and passion for digital design.
